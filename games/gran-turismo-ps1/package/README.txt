The MX-5 RF in Gran Turismo (PlayStation) - built on your PC (Windows)
=======================================================================

You need:
  - YOUR Gran Turismo, EUROPE multi-language version (PAL, SCES-00984), as a disc image:
    .chd, or .cue + .bin, or .bin;
  - the Assetto Corsa model of the MX-5 RF: mazda_mx5_rf.kn5 (free mod, see the project page).
No file of the game or of the model is included here.

1. Put your game image and the .kn5 file in this folder (next to FABRIQUER.bat).
2. Double-click FABRIQUER.bat.
   - The first time, without Python: it downloads an official portable copy of Python
     (python.org, about 30 MB) into outils\python. Nothing is installed in Windows.
3. Answer the questions: language, which Roadsters to replace (Enter = all),
   closed or open top, the price at the Mazda dealer (29900 = realistic; 100 = from day one).
4. About a minute later the disc is in MX5_RF_closed (or MX5_RF_open):
   open the .cue in DuckStation.

Your Gran Turismo save works with this disc (it is on the emulator's memory card).
Your game image is never modified.
